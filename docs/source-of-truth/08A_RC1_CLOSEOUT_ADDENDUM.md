## RC1 scope decisions

### MASTER_LEASE

MASTER_LEASE is **EXCLUDED from RC1**. Existing `gl_ml_*` kernels, migrations
and tests remain preserved, but RC1 must not expose a route, dashboard, report
or claim implying that the principal-accounting journey is available or
IFRS-complete. GAP-012 therefore remains a deferred capability gap, not an RC1
release blocker.

### Property onboarding

RC1 adopts the existing backend-driven **five-step** operating order:

1. Owner / authority identity
2. Property record
3. Unit record
4. Contract setup
5. Invoice / operating readiness

Owner/authority and property identity remain non-waivable. The future seven-step
property-type catalog and jurisdiction-specific safety/legal evidence model are
deferred. This is a Product Owner scope decision, not an Oman legal-sufficiency
claim.

### Adjustment events

RC1 includes only governed adjustment/reversal events whose accounting mapping
is already canonical and implemented. Receipt VOID, mapped invoice credit,
deposit refund/reversal, commission reversal and already-governed
owner-receivable/termination reversal behavior remain in scope.

Late fees/other-charge revenue, unmapped general non-deposit cash refunds and
unmapped non-cash adjustments are **EXCLUDED and must remain fail-closed** until
an Accountant approves their GL/tax mapping. No account is invented and the
canonical chart is not widened by this closeout.
